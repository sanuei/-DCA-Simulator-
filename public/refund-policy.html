1. General Policy / 一般政策

English: At DCA Simulator, we strive to provide high-quality services and ensure customer satisfaction. This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions under which refunds may be issued for purchases made through our Service.

2. Digital Products and Services / 数字产品和服务

English: Since our Service provides immediate access to digital content (Pro subscription features, activation codes), all sales are generally considered final. However, we may consider refunds in the following circumstances:

  • Technical Issues: If you are unable to access the Service due to technical problems on our end that we cannot resolve within 7 days
  • Duplicate Purchase: If you accidentally purchased the same product or service twice
  • Unauthorized Transaction: If your payment method was used without your authorization
  • Service Not Delivered: If you did not receive the activation code or access to Pro features within 48 hours of purchase

中文：由于我们的服务提供对数字内容的即时访问（Pro 订阅功能、激活码），所有销售通常被视为最终销售。但是，在以下情况下，我们可能会考虑退款：

  • 技术问题：如果您因我们无法在7天内解决的技术问题而无法访问服务
  • 重复购买：如果您意外购买了相同的产品或服务两次
  • 未经授权的交易：如果您的支付方式在未经您授权的情况下被使用
  • 服务未交付：如果您在购买后48小时内未收到激活码或访问 Pro 功能

English: Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of the original purchase date. Requests submitted after this period will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances apply.

English: The following are generally not eligible for refunds:

  • Purchases made more than 30 days ago
  • Refund requests due to change of mind or dissatisfaction with service features (unless service was not as described)

中文：以下项目通常不符合退款条件：
